Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about scoring matches with QuickRuns.

Is QuickRuns completely free to use?

Yes, QuickRuns is completely free. You can create unlimited matches, score unlimited overs, and generate live spectator links without paying any subscription fees.

Does QuickRuns work in remote cricket grounds without internet?

Yes! QuickRuns was engineered from day one with an offline-first architecture. You can score the full match even in remote grounds without network reception. Once your device detects internet, the match data syncs automatically.

Do spectators need to install the app to watch my match?

No! When you share the live link on WhatsApp, spectators can view the full live scorecard and ball-by-ball updates directly in any web browser without logging in or downloading anything.

Can I score Box Cricket or Turf Tournaments with special rules?

Yes. You can customize overs (e.g. 4, 6, 8, 12 overs), custom player dismissals, runs-per-wicket penalty, and wide/no-ball re-bowl rules to fit any local or turf tournament.

How can I recover my matches if I change or reinstall my phone?

QuickRuns links your matches to your device cloud identity. When reinstalling on the same device or recovering via cloud sync, your match history and squads can be restored with a single tap.
